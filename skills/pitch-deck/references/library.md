# Pitch reference library

What real pitches did, what investors say they want, and what studies measured. Use it to check a
draft against real decks of the same stage and kind, and to tell a measured rule from an opinion.
It does not replace the workflow in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md); it feeds the narrative, the slides,
the check of the draft and the objection prep.

**When the library and the workflow differ, the workflow wins.** Slide count per audience comes
from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) and slide order from [pitch-structure.md](pitch-structure.md). The
library shows what real decks did and says what is opinion; it does not override either. Tell
the founder where they differ: the catalog's decks carry a median of 14.5 content slides, for
example, against the 10 to 12 slides SKILL.md sets for a VC deck.

Everything third-party is linked, never copied: decks, videos, articles and papers stay at their
source, and quotes stay under 25 words. Counts, statistics and gradings are ours. Sources checked
2026-10-08; a link that could not be opened is marked unverified.

## What is in each file

| File | What it holds | Read it when |
|---|---|---|
| [library-teardown-ruler.md](library-teardown-ruler.md) | Words per slide, slides per deck, claim titles and deck types measured on 78 decks that raised; text thresholds taken from where the reviewer complains, which do not predict raising; and the method to reapply them | Sizing the text on each slide; deciding between a deck that is read and one that is presented |
| [library-teardown-catalog.md](library-teardown-catalog.md) | All 100 TechCrunch pitch deck teardowns: stage, sector, round, link, our counts, next funding event | Finding a real deck of your stage and sector to compare side by side |

## Where it plugs into the workflow

- **Slides:** the [ruler](library-teardown-ruler.md) for text.
- **Checking the draft:** put it side by side with two or three real decks of the same stage from
  the [catalog](library-teardown-catalog.md).

## Caveats that apply to every file

- **Survivorship.** Almost every example raised money or won something; none of these files has a
  control group. A pattern here is something winners did, not proof that it made them win.
- **Dates.** Rounds and outcomes were sourced between 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-08. Re-open a source
  before quoting it to an investor.
