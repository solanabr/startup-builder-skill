# Storytelling frameworks

Pick one backbone for the deck and say why. The framework is the story; [pitch-structure.md](pitch-structure.md) is the container. All numbers below are placeholders to show the shape.

## Choosing

1. The strongest asset is a working demo → **Before-After-Bridge**
2. Raising from VCs → **6-part investor arc**
3. The pain is obvious and visceral → **PAS**
4. You have a real user story → **Hero's journey**
5. The product is technically hard to grasp → **Pixar**
6. None of the above, or a non-technical marketing audience → **AIDA**

Layering is fine: PAS for the first three slides, then the investor arc for the body.

## PAS: problem, agitate, solve

Hackathons and early teams with a sharp problem insight but thin traction.

| Beat | Example shape |
|---|---|
| Problem | "Agents on Solana can't pay each other without a human co-signer." |
| Agitate | "So every agent action waits on a person. At [N] actions a day, that is [N] bottlenecks." |
| Solve | "[Product]: agents settle with each other onchain, no human in the loop." |

Slides 1–2 problem and agitation, 3–4 solution and why crypto, 5 the demo that proves it.

## 6-part investor arc

The strongest backbone for a raise. Investors fund inevitabilities, so you ride a shift rather than claim to create one.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| The shift | A large change happening now ("agents are moving from reading to transacting") |
| The obstacle | What stops the world adapting ("wallet infrastructure assumes a human signer") |
| The tool | Your product as the way through |
| The proof | Traction that validates the thesis, with sources |
| The future | The world if you win |
| The invitation | The ask, framed as joining |

Expand "the proof" into traction, market and business model for a VC deck.

## Before-After-Bridge

Product-led teams with a demo that shows a visible change.

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Before | A specific person doing the painful thing today: four DEX tabs, three approvals, eight minutes |
| After | The same person with your product: one input, one signature |
| Bridge | How the product gets them there |

The gap between before and after is the value proposition. Measure it on your own product.

## Hero's journey: the user is the hero

Consumer products with real user stories. The user is the hero; you are the guide.

Ordinary world (a freelancer losing a cut of every cross-border invoice) → call to adventure (a client insists on paying in USDC) → the guide appears (your product) → trials (the demo: receive, convert, off-ramp) → transformation (what is measurably better) → return (the scale of people like them).

## Pixar

Infrastructure and other products whose "how" is hard to explain.

"Once upon a time… Every day… One day… Because of that… Because of that… Until finally…" — roughly one beat per slide. The "one day" beat is your why-now: name the specific change that shipped.

## AIDA

Attention (one slide), interest (about five), desire (about three), action (one). Weaker tension than the others; better for launches and non-technical audiences than for a raise.
